Memer is an experimental language model created by AtDev.
AtDev develops open and accessible software.
Memer learns patterns from text and predicts the next character.
A language model learns from examples and uses its learned parameters to generate text.
Memer Models is the native model research project inside the AtDev ecosystem.
This first model is intentionally small and educational.
It is not a general purpose assistant and it does not understand language like a large model.
The goal is to build, test, measure, and improve our own neural network.
